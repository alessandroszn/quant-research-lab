

## Deviations

- **The study sections were drafted in parallel.** Four helper agents each condensed one report
  into its section and its values file. Each checked its values against the records and
  reported the numbers it left out. Then:
  - I spot-checked at least three values per study directly against the tables;
  - I wrote the abstract, introduction, methods, discussion, limitations, conclusion,
    references and appendix.
- **The reports had last-digit errors.** Generating the paper from the records found 13 figures
  in the four reports that differed in the last digit, mostly from rounding twice, and one
  overstated claim ("lowest Sharpe ratio", study 2). The reports are corrected in the same
  change and listed in the CHANGELOG.
- **Very small p-values.** They are printed as "< 0.0001", computed from the value by a `below`
  option of the generator, instead of in scientific notation, which pdflatex cannot typeset
  from Unicode superscripts.
- **The commit caveat.** Every study record shows uncommitted changes at run time. Appendix A
  says so, and a clean re-run is recorded in `docs/IDEAS.md`.
- **MiKTeX downloads without asking.** While checking which LaTeX packages were missing, my
  `kpsewhich` calls triggered MiKTeX's automatic installer, which on this machine installs
  without asking. 18 packages were downloaded from MiKTeX's repository before I noticed; I
  reported this to the owner. Later builds use `pdflatex --disable-installer`, so nothing is
  installed. The PDF still needs at least one more package (`letltxmacro`), whose installation
  is the owner's decision.
