# Design note — Phase 8.6b: Italian losses in projections and backtests

Status: **draft, waiting for a decision** (implement now, or park in IDEAS). Deviations, if any,
are listed at the end. It follows 8.6a (`docs/design/8.6-italian-tax.md`).

## What happens today

Both engines tax realised gains with one flat rate (`qrlab.simulation.TaxModel`):

- **Projections** (`qrlab.simulation.plan.project`) tax the gains realised by rebalancing and
  withdrawals.
- **Backtests** (`qrlab.backtest.engine`) tax the gains realised when they trade.

In both, a realised loss is ignored: `np.clip(gains, 0, None)`. It does not reduce the tax on
another asset's gain, and nothing is carried to later years. The methodology page says this
overstates taxes slightly.

## What the Italian rules would change

From 8.6a:

- **Gains on ETFs and funds** are *redditi di capitale*: taxed in full, never reduced by
  losses.
- **Losses on any asset,** ETFs included, are *redditi diversi*: they offset only gains on
  shares, bonds and ETCs, for four years.

**For a portfolio of ETFs only, as yours is, the rules change no number.** The ETF gains are
taxed in full, which is what happens today. The ETF losses would go into a balance that
nothing can use, so they would only expire. The tax paid is the same to the cent. The change
matters only when a projection or a backtest holds shares, bonds or ETCs next to funds.

## Design, if implemented

1. **`TaxModel` gains an optional `losses: LossRules | None = None`.**
   - `None` keeps today's behaviour: losses ignored.
   - `LossRules` has:
     - `carry_years` (4 for Italy);
     - `fund_assets`, the assets whose gains are capital income and so use no losses.

   The field is an addition with a default, so existing configs and studies are unchanged.
2. **Per path,** in projections, a balance of losses by year.
   - It is an array of paths × years, filled when a sale realises a loss and used, oldest
     first, by gains on non-fund assets.
   - Losses expire after `carry_years`.
   - Years come from `periods_per_year`.
3. **In backtests,** the same balance on the net ledger, by calendar year of the execution
   date.
4. **The terminal liquidation tax** of a projection uses the balance left for non-fund gains.
5. **The 12.5 % share of government bonds** stays out, as in the engines today. Fund-level
   public-bond shares are an analytics input, not a projection assumption.

### Tests (hand-calculated first)

- **Funds only.** A projection and a backtest of ETFs only, run with and without
  `LossRules`, give byte-identical results.
- **A share and a fund.** A realised loss of 1,000 on the share in year 1, and gains of 600
  on the fund and 600 on the share in year 2:
  - the fund's 600 is taxed in full (156);
  - the share's 600 uses 600 of the loss (tax 0);
  - 400 is carried.
- **Expiry.** A loss from year 1 is no longer usable in year 6.
- **Backtest.** A two-asset backtest with one rebalancing at a loss and a later one at a gain,
  worked by hand.
