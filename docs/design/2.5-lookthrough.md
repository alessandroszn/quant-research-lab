# Design note — Phase 2.5 `qrlab.lookthrough`: look-through and ETF analysis

Status: **approved 2026-11-11.** Deviations, if any, are listed at the end.

## Goal

See what the portfolio holds through its ETFs:

- exposures by country, sector, currency and security, with every holdings date shown;
- overlap between funds;
- tracking difference, tracking error, and tracking difference against the TER;
- the 9 health checks that need these numbers, added to the 2.4 engine;
- a look-through section in the portfolio report.

With this phase, M2 "Portfolio X-ray" is complete.

## Inputs

All inputs come from files the user downloads into the git-ignored `data/` folder. No new data
source and no download code, so no new terms-of-use review.

- **Holdings:** one `HoldingsSnapshot` per fund (phase 1.3), read from the issuer's file
  through a declared mapping. Each snapshot has its own `as_of` date.
- **Fund and index return series** for tracking difference:
  - user CSVs (phase 1.2);
  - the fund series must be NAV or total return, and the index series total return (net
    dividends);
  - a price-only series is refused, because it leaves dividends out.
- **Portfolio weights:** from the 2.1 valuation on `as_of`, including cash, as in 2.4.
- **Optional issuer map** (TOML, security ISIN → issuer name). Without it, concentration is
  measured per security and labelled "security, not issuer". Alphabet class A and C, for
  example, are two securities of one issuer.

## Definitions

### Exposures

- Fund $f$ has portfolio weight $w_f$, and holding $i$ of fund $f$ has weight $h_{f,i}$.
  - The look-through weight of holding $i$ is $w_f\,h_{f,i}$.
  - Exposures sum these weights by country, sector, currency or security.
- **Direct positions** (a stock or bond held directly) look through to themselves:
  - country: the master-data `domicile`;
  - currency: the instrument currency;
  - sector: unknown unless master data gains it, shown as "unknown".
- **Cash** is its own bucket, in its own currency.
- **Reported weights are not rescaled by default.** The treatment is an explicit parameter,
  `weights = "as_reported" | "normalised"`:
  - `"as_reported"`: if a fund's holdings sum to 98 %, the missing 2 % of $w_f$ goes to an
    "unexplained" bucket;
  - `"normalised"`: uses `normalised_weights` (phase 1.3) and says so in the report.
- **Unknown values** (no country, sector or currency for a holding) go to an "unknown" bucket.
  They are never spread over the known ones.
- **Funds without a snapshot** go to a "not looked through" bucket, with their weight shown.
- **Currency exposure** uses the currency of each holding. A hedged share class is shown at its
  hedge currency, with the underlying currencies listed separately. Hedging removes most, but
  not all, of the currency effect.
- **Dates:** each fund's snapshot date is shown next to its exposures. Mixing snapshots from
  different dates is allowed but listed.

### Overlap

- **Pairwise overlap** of funds $f$ and $g$ is $\sum_i \min(h_{f,i}, h_{g,i})$ over securities
  matched by ISIN.
  - Two identical funds have overlap 1; funds with no common security have overlap 0.
  - Holdings without an ISIN cannot be matched; their share is reported next to the overlap.
- **Duplicated exposure** is the look-through weight of securities held through two or more
  funds. It answers how much of the portfolio sits in securities bought more than once.

### Tracking

- **Tracking difference** over a period is the fund's total return (or NAV return) minus the
  index total return over the same dates.
  - It is computed per calendar year and over the whole common period, annualised
    geometrically when the period is at least a year.
  - It is never annualised from less than a year.
- **Tracking error** is the annualised sample standard deviation of the periodic return
  differences, with `periods_per_year` explicit. The fund and index series must share an index
  (`require_same_index`, no silent alignment).
- **Tracking difference vs. TER** $= \text{TD} + \text{TER}$:
  - 0 means the fund lost exactly its TER;
  - a negative value means hidden costs beyond the TER;
  - a positive value means extra income, such as securities lending or tax effects.
- **Currency:** the fund series and the index must be in the same currency, or the index must
  be hedged to the fund's hedge currency. Otherwise the calculation raises, because a currency
  effect would be read as tracking.
- **NAV vs. market price:** NAV-based tracking measures the fund. Market-price tracking adds
  the bid-ask spread and premium or discount. The series type is shown in the report.

## Checks added to the 2.4 engine (9)

| Id | Direction | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| `lookthrough.country` | above | largest country weight through holdings |
| `lookthrough.sector` | above | largest sector weight |
| `lookthrough.issuer` | above | largest issuer weight (per security without an issuer map, labelled) |
| `lookthrough.pair_overlap` | above | largest pairwise overlap (redundant funds), per pair |
| `lookthrough.duplicated` | above | duplicated exposure |
| `etf_quality.tracking_difference` | below | annualised TD per fund |
| `etf_quality.tracking_error` | above | annualised TE per fund |
| `etf_quality.td_vs_ter` | below | TD + TER per fund (hidden costs) |
| `data_quality.stale_holdings` | above | snapshots older than `max_age_days` (count) |

The 2.4 note counted 8. The correct count is 9, because tracking difference and tracking error
are two checks. The registry goes from 27 to 36. "Unknown" and "unexplained" buckets are never
counted as one country, sector or issuer.

`CheckContext` gains a `lookthrough: LookThrough | None` input. `None` makes these checks not
applicable, with the reason, as in 2.4.

## Edge cases

- **Holdings:** a fund with no snapshot; an empty snapshot; duplicated holdings (reported by
  `holdings_issues`, summed with a note); weights above 100 % (leverage or derivatives: shown,
  never capped).
- **Holding types:** cash and derivatives inside fund holdings, kept under their asset class;
  a fund of funds (one level only, with a note).
- **Portfolio:** a portfolio of only cash; only direct stocks.
- **Series:** fund and index series with different dates (raise); fewer than 2 return
  differences (TE not applicable).

## Risks

- **Issuer files differ widely.** The declared mapping (1.3) absorbs format differences.
  Country names instead of codes need the country table listed in `docs/IDEAS.md`; in this
  phase they are refused with a clear message.
- **Snapshots are dated, the portfolio is not.** Exposures mix the holdings date and the
  valuation date; the report shows both and flags stale snapshots.
- **Index data may be licensed.** It is read only from the user's own files and never
  committed.
- **Tracking from market prices** mixes in spreads and premiums. The series type is a required
  input and is shown.
