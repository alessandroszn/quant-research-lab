# Design note — Phase 9.3: a configurable tax model for other countries

Status: **approved 2026-11-15.** Deviations, if any, are listed at the
end. Third phase of Level 9 (`docs/design/level-9-plan.md`), on the shape of 9.1.

## Goal

A first view of the taxes of any country not modelled in detail. You state the few rules that
matter, and qrlab applies them to the account.

- **It is labelled everywhere** as "a simplified model you configure, not a country's law",
  in the report section, its notice and the docs.
- **Nothing is assumed:** every key is required, so a forgotten setting is an error, never a
  silent default.

## Settings: `[parameters.tax]` with `country = "custom"`

| Key | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `description` | your name for the model, shown in the report (e.g. "Spain, my reading") |
| `gains_rate` | rate on taxable gains, in [0, 1) |
| `income_rate` | rate on dividends and interest (gross), in [0, 1) |
| `losses` | `"ignored"`: a loss never reduces tax (each gain taxed alone); `"netted"`: the year's gains and losses are netted |
| `loss_carry_years` | with `"netted"`: how many following years a net loss can be used (0: that year only); must be 0 with `"ignored"` |
| `exempt_after_months` | gains on units held at least this many months are exempt; 0 means never exempt |
| `gains_allowance` | yearly amount of taxable gains that is tax-free, in the base currency (0 for none) |
| `wealth_tax_rate` | yearly rate on the account's year-end value, in [0, 1) |

The base currency is any, because the amounts are in the report's base currency.

## Rules, in this order, per calendar year

1. **Each sale's result** is the ledger's realised gain or loss, at average cost.
2. **Exemption by holding period.** The units sold are matched first in, first out, as in
   9.2. The share of the units held at least `exempt_after_months` is exempt, and that share
   of the sale's result is taken out.
   - This is an approximation: the cost is the average, while the ages come from first in,
     first out. The report says so.
   - 0 months means nothing is exempt.
3. **Losses.**
   - `"ignored"`: the taxable amount is the sum of the year's taxable gains.
   - `"netted"`: the year's taxable gains and losses are netted. A net loss joins a balance
     usable for `loss_carry_years` following years, oldest first. A net gain is then reduced
     by that balance.
4. **Allowance.** `gains_allowance` is taken off what is left, never below 0.
5. **Gains tax** = `gains_rate` × the rest. **Income tax** = `income_rate` × the gross
   dividends and interest. **Wealth tax** = `wealth_tax_rate` × the value at the last
   valuation of the year, for years that have ended.
6. **Tax withheld** on income is shown next to the computed income tax. It is not credited,
   because whether it can be depends on the country and the treaty.

Taxes are rounded to the cent, half up.

## Outputs

**Tables** (the 9.1 shape):

- **`custom_years`**, one row per year:
  - gains, losses and the exempt part;
  - losses used and the allowance used;
  - taxable gains and the gains tax;
  - income, its tax and the tax withheld;
  - wealth and the wealth tax.
- **`custom_losses`**, written only with `"netted"`: the loss balance by year, with its amount,
  used, expired, remaining and "usable until". It is the same table as Italy's, with the
  carry period you state.
- **In the report only:** your settings, and the model's label.

**Scalars:** for the latest complete year, `gains_tax_estimate`, `income_tax_estimate`,
`wealth_tax_estimate` and `losses_remaining`.

**Projections:** a library preset `custom_tax_model(settings, taxable_yield=...)`:

- the gains rate;
- the wealth-tax rate;
- `LossRules(carry, fund_assets=∅)` when losses are netted, so every asset's losses count;
- the income rate × the taxable yield you give.

The preset ignores the exemption by holding period and the allowance, which `TaxModel`
does not model; the docs say so.

## Tests, hand-calculated first

EUR account; `gains_rate` 20 %, `income_rate` 25 %, `"netted"` with 2 carry years, exemption
after 12 months, allowance 1,000, wealth tax 0.

- **2025.** Buy 100 X at 100 on 2 January; sell 50 at 80 on 2 June (5 months): a loss of 1,000.
  It is carried, usable until 2027. Gains tax 0.
- **2026.** Buy 100 Y at 50 on 5 January.
  - Sell the other 50 X at 150 on 1 February: a gain of 2,500, held 13 months, so exempt.
  - Y pays a dividend of 400, with 60 withheld, on 15 February.
  - Sell the 100 Y at 80 on 1 March: a gain of 3,000, held 2 months, so taxable.
  - Taxable 3,000 − 1,000 carried − 1,000 allowance = 1,000, so the gains tax is 200.
  - Income tax 25 % × 400 = 100, with 60 withheld shown.
- **The same account, `"ignored"`:** 2026 is 3,000 − 1,000 allowance = 2,000, so the tax is
  400.
- **The same account, `"netted"` with 0 carry years:** the 2025 loss is not carried, so 2026
  pays 400.
- **A partly exempt sale:** 150 units sold out of 100 old and 50 new, so 2/3 of the result is
  exempt.
- **Allowance above the gains:** the tax is 0, never negative.
- **Errors:**
  - a missing key;
  - `loss_carry_years` other than 0 with `"ignored"`;
  - a rate out of range;
  - an unknown `losses` value;
  - a negative allowance.
- **Report:** the label "a simplified model you configure, not a country's law" is in the
  section, in English and Italian. Italian and Swiss outputs are unchanged.

## Risks

- **Taken for a country's law.** The label is in the section title and the notice, and your
  `description` is shown.
- **The FIFO and average-cost mix** for exemptions is an approximation, stated in the report.
- **Order of losses and allowance.** Countries differ; this note fixes one order (netting,
  then carried losses, then allowance), and the report states it.

## Deviations

- **The run's notice is a field of the shape:** `TaxReport.notice`, which the report kind
  appends. Italy and Switzerland have none, so their notices are unchanged.
- **First in, first out moved to `qrlab.tax.lots`** (`fifo_sales`, `add_months`), shared by
  Switzerland and this model. The Swiss outputs are identical to `main`'s.
- **A unit is held long enough** when its acquisition date plus the months is on or before
  the sale date, per lot, as in Switzerland.
- **The section title itself** says "a simplified model you configured, not a country's law"
  (`custom_title`), besides the paragraphs and the notice.
- **`checked_on` is the report date.** There is no law to check, and the sources sentence
  says the settings are yours.
- **Byte-identity check.** The seven examples, eight Italian runs and two Swiss runs, run with
  the code of `main` and of this branch, are identical.
