# Design note 11.3 — Italian stamp duty and IVAFE

Status: **approved 2026-11-16.** Third phase of Level 11 (`docs/design/levels-10-13-plan.md`).

## The problem

The Italian tax section (8.6) covers the tax on gains. An Italian resident also pays a yearly
tax on the **value** of the financial products held:

- **Held through an Italian intermediary:** the *imposta di bollo* on the periodic statements.
- **Held abroad:** the *IVAFE*, paid in the tax return.

For a long-term ETF portfolio this is often the largest tax while nothing is sold: 0.2 % a
year of the value, every year. The report shows nothing of it today.

## The rules, as checked on 16 November 2026

Sources: the official instructions *Redditi Persone Fisiche 2026, fascicolo 2, quadro RW*
(tax year 2025), Agenzia delle Entrate circolare 48/E of 21 December 2012, and the laws they
cite.

### Imposta di bollo (D.P.R. 642/1972, tariffa parte I, art. 13, comma 2-ter, and nota 3-ter)

- **Rate:** 2 per mille a year on the statements of financial products, from 2014. It was
  raised from 1.5 per mille by L. 147/2013, art. 1, comma 581, which also abolished the
  €34.20 minimum on these statements.
- **The €14,000 cap** applies only to clients other than natural persons, so not here.
- **Base:** the market value of the products at the end of each statement period or, failing
  it, their nominal or redemption value, or else their cost (D.M. 24 May 2012, art. 3).
- **The year:** it is the calendar year. With statements sent during the year, the tax is
  proportional to the days of each period: circolare 48/E works it as
  $V \times r \times \text{days}/365$.
  - With no statement, the tax applies on 31 December and when the account is closed.
  - **Products sold during a period** have no value at its end, so they pay nothing for that
    period.
- **Rounding** (D.P.R. 642/1972, art. 3, comma 2, as quoted in circolare 48/E): to €0.10, down
  for a fraction up to €0.05 and up above it.
- **Minimum** (D.P.R. 642/1972, art. 3, comma 3): €1 per statement.
- **Products held abroad but custodied by an Italian intermediary** pay the stamp duty, not
  the IVAFE (circolare 48/E).
- **Both tax regimes:** the stamp duty is due whatever the regime for gains (*amministrato* or
  *dichiarativo*), because it depends on the custodian.

### IVAFE (D.L. 201/2011, art. 19, commi 18–22)

- **Rate:** 0.20 % a year of the value of financial products held abroad.
  - **0.40 % for products held in states or territories with a privileged tax regime** (the
    list of D.M. 4 May 1999), from 2024 (L. 213/2023, art. 1, comma 91). In the return this
    is RW column 21.
- **Base:** the quoted value on 31 December or at the end of the holding period. For
  unquoted securities: nominal value, otherwise redemption value.
- **Proportional to the days held and to the ownership share** (RW columns 10 and 5).
- **Securities of the same kind** bought at different times and partly sold: the ones bought
  most recently count as sold first (LIFO).
- **Amounts in foreign currency** are converted at the Agenzia's monthly average rate of the
  month (provvedimento on the *cambi medi mensili*).
- **Credit:** a wealth tax paid abroad on the same products is deducted, up to the IVAFE.
- **Payment:** with IRPEF. It is not paid if the year's balance (row RW6, column 5) is €12
  or less.
- **Current accounts and savings books abroad** pay a fixed €34.20 instead: out of scope here.

### What is not in force

- **D.Lgs. 123/2025** (testo unico on the stamp duty and other indirect taxes) moves both
  taxes into new articles. Art. 205 makes it apply from **1 January 2027**; D.L. 200/2025,
  art. 4, comma 5, moved that date from 1 January 2026.
- Commentaries say it reorders the rules without changing them, but I have not checked the
  new articles.
- **So 2027 and later years are not computed:** the section says so, as 8.6 does for the
  years it does not cover.
- **Years before 2014** had other rates, and are not computed either.

## The change

### 1. Where each account is held: a new optional key in the Italian `[parameters.tax]`

```toml
[parameters.tax.custody."Bank A"]       # the account name, as in the ledger
where = "italy"                         # the stamp duty
statements = "quarter"                  # "year", "quarter" or "month"

[parameters.tax.custody."Broker B"]
where = "abroad"                        # the IVAFE; "abroad_privileged" for 0.40 %
share = 1.0                             # the owner's share of the account
```

- **Without `custody`** the section is unchanged, byte for byte.
- **With it,** every account that holds securities in a year must be listed. An account not
  listed raises, and so does an unknown key or value.
- **`statements` is required for `italy`,** and refused for the others. **`share` is required
  for the IVAFE,** in $(0, 1]$, and refused for `italy`.
- **Cash is not covered:** the fixed amounts on current accounts depend on average balances
  that the ledger does not have.

### 2. The computation, per complete calendar year (from 2014 to 2026)

**Stamp duty,** per `italy` account and per statement period $p$ of the year (12 months, 4
quarters or 1 year):

$$\text{tax}_p = \max\bigl(1,\ \operatorname{round}_{0.10}(0.002 \times V_p \times d_p / 365)\bigr)
\quad\text{if } V_p > 0, \text{ else } 0$$

- $V_p$ is the value of the account's securities at the period's end.
- $d_p$ counts the days of the period during which the account was open, from its first
  transaction.
- **A whole year on yearly statements** counts as exactly one year, so a leap year does not
  pay 366/365 of the rate.

**IVAFE,** per `abroad` account, per holding and per lot. The lots come from the account's
transactions, matched LIFO.

$$\text{tax} = \operatorname{round}_{0.01}\Bigl(\sum_{\text{lots}} r \times q \times P_{\text{end}} \times \frac{\text{days}}{\text{days in the year}} \times s\Bigr)$$

- **The rate $r$** is 0.002, or 0.004 for `abroad_privileged` from 2024.
- **The lot's units $q$** are valued at $P_{\text{end}}$, the market price on 31 December, or
  on the day the lot was sold.
- **The days:** a unit is held on each day at whose end it is in the account. So it counts
  from the purchase day, and not on the day of sale.
- **The share $s$** is the owner's share of the account.
- **Dividing by the days in the year** (365 or 366) gives a whole year exactly the annual
  rate. In leap years this differs from a reading with 365 by at most 1/365 of the tax, and
  the note says so.

**Prices and rates** come from the report's own data.
- The price is the last one on or before the date, within `max_price_age_days`. On 31
  December many exchanges are closed, so a too-small limit raises and says why.
- Foreign prices are converted with the report's ECB rate of that date. The return uses the
  Agenzia's monthly average rates instead, so the section says the difference is one of
  rates, a few basis points of the tax.

**Per year,** the totals of each tax, and for the IVAFE whether the year's total is above the
€12 payment threshold.

- **The foreign wealth-tax credit** is not applied (no input carries it), and the section
  says so.
- **The current year** is computed only once it ends.

### 3. Outputs

- **`wealth_tax_items.csv`,** one row per stamp-duty period or per IVAFE holding:
  - `year`, `account`, `tax` (`stamp_duty` or `ivafe`) and `holding`;
  - `period_end`, `value`, `days`, `rate` and `amount`.
- **`wealth_tax_years.csv`,** one row per year: `stamp_duty`, `ivafe`, and `ivafe_payable`
  (0 at or below €12).
- **The scalars,** for the last complete year: `stamp_duty_last_year` and `ivafe_last_year`.
- **The report:** two tables in the Italian tax section, in English and Italian, with the
  rules, the sources and the date they were checked, through the 9.1 shape. No new rendering
  code is needed.

## Edge cases (each raises, naming the account, the year or the date)

- **Custody:** an account with securities that is not in `custody`; an unknown `where`; a
  missing or refused `statements` or `share`; a share outside $(0, 1]$.
- **Prices:** no price within `max_price_age_days` at a period end or on a sale date.
- **Units:** a sale of more units than the account holds. The ledger already refuses that;
  the lot matching asserts it too.
- **Years:** a year from 2027 is not computed and is noted; a year before 2014 is not computed
  either.

## Tests, hand-calculated first

1. **Yearly stamp duty.** €10,000 at 31 December 2025, account open all year: $0.002 \times
   10{,}000 = €20.00$.
2. **Quarterly stamp duty,** with circolare 48/E's own pattern. €10,000 every quarter of
   2025:
   - $20 \times 90/365 = 4.93 \to 4.90$;
   - $20 \times 91/365 = 4.99 \to 5.00$;
   - $20 \times 92/365 = 5.04 \to 5.00$, twice;
   - total €19.90.
3. **Rounding and minimum:**
   - the circolare's 2013 figures at 1.5 per mille, as a regression of the rounding:
     3.6986 → 3.70, 3.7397 → 3.70, 3.7808 → 3.80;
   - 4.05 → 4.00 and 4.0501 → 4.10;
   - an account opened on 20 December with €1,000 owes $0.0658 \to €1.00$.
4. **IVAFE, one lot:** 100 units bought on 1 March 2025, priced 50 on 31 December. That is
   306 days, so $0.002 \times 5{,}000 \times 306/365 = €8.38$.
5. **IVAFE, LIFO.** 100 units bought on 2 January and 50 on 1 July; 60 sold on 1 October.
   - **Matching:** the 50 from July go first, then 10 from January. So 90 units are held from
     2 January to the end of the year; 10 from 2 January to 1 October, priced on 1 October;
     and 50 from 1 July to 1 October.
   - Each lot is computed by hand, and the total is checked against a day-by-day sum.
6. **Rates, share and threshold:**
   - `abroad_privileged` is 0.40 % in 2024 and 0.20 % in 2023;
   - a share of 0.5 halves the tax;
   - a year totalling €11.80 is payable 0, one totalling €12.01 is payable €12.01.
7. **Without `custody`,** the section and every output are as today (a regression test, and
   byte identity against `main`).
8. **Each error above,** and the tables in both languages.

## Risks

- **Statement periods differ by intermediary.** The owner states them. A mistaken period
  changes the stamp duty by the rounding and by the value path, not by its order of
  magnitude.
- **The returned IVAFE uses the Agenzia's monthly average rates** and the intermediary's
  statement value. The section is labelled as an estimate, like the *dichiarativo* gains
  (8.6).
- **The rules may change:** the section prints the date they were checked, and the years from
  2027 wait for the testo unico to be read.

## Out of scope (in `IDEAS.md`)

- The fixed €34.20 amounts on current accounts, in Italy and abroad.
- The foreign wealth-tax credit.
- Co-ownership of the stamp duty.
- Accounts of entities (the €14,000 cap).
- Insurance policies.
- Years from 2027, until the testo unico is checked.

## Results and deviations (2026-11-16)

- **Built as designed:**
  - `qrlab.tax.italy_wealth`, with `parse_custody`, `round_stamp_duty`, `lifo_lots` and
    `wealth_taxes`;
  - the optional `custody` key of the Italian regime;
  - two tables, two CSV files and two scalars in the tax section, in English and Italian.
- **The report's prices reach the tax code:** `TaxInputs` gains `prices` and
  `max_price_age_days`, both optional and both additions to the public API. The report kind
  passes them. The tax section could not value holdings on 31 December or on a sale date from
  the valuation dates alone.
- **An account opens on its first transaction,** of any kind. The days of its first statement
  period count from that date.
- **A holding with several lots** shows the sum of the lots' values, and the value-weighted
  average of their days, as the RW instructions ask when lines are aggregated. The tax is
  computed per lot and rounded once per holding, to the cent.
- **The stamp duty's €1 minimum** applies only to a period that ends with securities in the
  account. A period with none pays nothing, as the minimum of €34.20 on such periods was
  abolished in 2014.
- **The sentences on stamp duty and IVAFE** come before the sources line of the section, and
  their sources are added to the section's sources in both languages.
- **Tests:**
  - 21 unit tests: every hand value of this note (€20.00, €19.90, €1.60, €1.00, €8.38,
    €10.78, the circolare's rounding), a split, a lot sold in an earlier year, the years not
    computed, and every error;
  - 5 report tests: the stamp duty and the IVAFE by hand in both languages, two custody
    errors, and the section unchanged without `custody`.
- **The gate:** 2616 tests on Python 3.13 and 3.11, with 100 % coverage; the docs and their
  doctests build. The 3.11 environment needed the known reinstall for the `packaging` stubs.
- **Byte identity against `main`:** every output of the 8 example configs is equal, since none
  uses `custody`.
