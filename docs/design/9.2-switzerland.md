# Design note — Phase 9.2: Switzerland, private investor

Status: **approved 2026-11-15.** Deviations, if any, are listed at the
end. Second phase of Level 9 (`docs/design/level-9-plan.md`), on the shape of 9.1
(`qrlab.tax.TaxRegime`).

## The rules, checked on 2026-11-15

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| **Gains on private wealth are not taxable.** | LIFD (SR 642.11) art. 16 cpv. 3, version of 2 September 2026; the cantons the same (LAID art. 7 cpv. 4 lett. b). |
| **Interest and dividends are income** (gross, before any withholding). | LIFD art. 20 cpv. 1 lett. a and c. |
| **A fund's income belongs to its investors.** An accumulating fund's reinvested income is taxed every year. For a foreign fund the moment is the fund's yearly closing, and the amount is the gross income per unit in the ESTV price list. | LIFD art. 10 cpv. 2; ESTV Circular 25; BGE 151 II 418 (9C_757/2023, 9 December 2024): CHF 11.789 per unit from the price list was upheld, and the investor was taxed for the whole year although she bought in November. |
| **Swiss withholding tax 35 %** on Swiss dividends and interest; refunded when declared. | LVP/VStG (SR 642.21) art. 4 and 13 (to be read in full for the phase). |
| **Professional securities trading** is excluded when five criteria are all met. Otherwise it "cannot be excluded" and is judged case by case, with gains then taxable as self-employment income. | ESTV Circular No. 36 of 27 July 2012, section 3, read in the primary text. |
| **Wealth tax:** cantonal, on the wealth at the end of the period. | LAID art. 13 ff.; rates set by each canton. |

The five criteria of Circular 36:

1. **Holding period.** The securities sold were held at least 6 months.
2. **Volume.** Purchases plus sales in the calendar year total no more than 5 times the
   securities and cash at the start of the period.
3. **Gains.** Realised gains are below 50 % of net income in the period.
4. **Debt.** The investments are not debt-financed, or the taxable income from the securities
   exceeds the share of interest paid on debt.
5. **Derivatives.** Derivatives are only used to hedge one's own positions.

## What qrlab computes, and what you state

`[parameters.tax]` with `country = "ch"`:

| Key | What it is | Why it is yours |
|---|---|---|
| `marginal_rate` | combined federal, cantonal and municipal marginal income rate, in [0, 1) | depends on canton, municipality, income and family |
| `wealth_tax_rate` | cantonal and municipal wealth-tax rate, as a fraction (0.003 for 3 ‰) | depends on canton, municipality and total wealth |
| `net_income` | your net income per year, a table `2026 = 85000` (may be empty) | criterion 3 needs it; a year without it is "not checked" |
| `debt_financed` | `true` or `false`: are your investments financed by loans outside this account? | criterion 4; the account itself never has negative cash |
| `reported_income` | per accumulating fund, the price-list income per unit at each yearly closing: `IE00… = [{ date = 2025-12-31, per_unit = "2.345", currency = "CHF" }]` | the ESTV price list (ICTax), which you look up |

**Base currency.** Swiss taxes are in CHF, so the report needs `base_currency = "CHF"`;
otherwise it raises.

**The report section** (the 9.1 shape: sentences and tables, in English and Italian):

1. **Gains.** The realised gains per year, with tax 0 as private wealth, then the Circular 36
   check per year: one row per criterion, with the value, the threshold and met, not met or
   not checked.
   - **Holding period** of each sale: the age of the units sold, taken first in, first out.
     That is the most cautious reading under average cost.
   - **Volume:** the account's purchases and sales against its value (securities and cash)
     at the end of the previous year. The first year starts from its first valuation, and a
     note says so.
   - **Gains against net income:** from `net_income`, or not checked.
   - **Debt:** from `debt_financed`; the account itself has no negative cash.
   - **Derivatives:** qrlab has no derivative instruments, so the criterion is met, and the
     report says why.
   - **Conclusion:**
     - all five met: "private wealth management (ESTV safe haven): gains are not taxed";
     - otherwise: "professional trading cannot be excluded; the tax office judges the
       whole case (Circular 36, section 4)".

     The analytics never say "you are a professional trader".
2. **Income per year:**
   - dividends and interest at their gross amount;
   - the accumulating funds' reported income: per-unit amount × units held at the closing
     date, converted to CHF at that date;
   - the total, and the income tax estimated at `marginal_rate`.
3. **Swiss withholding tax:**
   - the tax withheld on income from Swiss-domiciled instruments, shown as refundable when
     declared;
   - foreign withholding tax is listed but not credited: the treaty relief (DA-1) is in
     IDEAS.
4. **Wealth per year:** the account's value at the last valuation on or before 31 December,
   and the tax at `wealth_tax_rate`. The price list's year-end prices may differ slightly from
   your price files; the report says so.

**Scalars:**

- `gains_realised` and `safe_haven_met` (1 or 0, latest year);
- `income_taxable` and `income_tax_estimate`;
- `withholding_refundable`;
- `wealth` and `wealth_tax_estimate`, for the latest year-end.

### Projections and backtests (the presets deferred from 9.1)

- **`TaxModel` gains an optional yearly tax on income:** `income_tax_rate` × `taxable_yield`,
  a fraction of value paid each period like the wealth tax.
  - For accumulating funds this is exact: their reinvested income is taxed every year.
  - For distributing funds it is an approximation, because projections use total returns.
- **The projection in the report** gains two optional keys, `income_tax_rate` and
  `taxable_yield`, both or neither.
- **A library preset,** `swiss_tax_model(wealth_tax_rate=…, income_tax_rate=…,
  taxable_yield=…)`, sets the gains rate to 0.
- **Without the new keys** every projection is unchanged, byte for byte.

## Tests, hand-calculated first

A CHF account, synthetic, with an accumulating fund A, a Swiss distributing fund B, and
`marginal_rate` 0.30.

- **Income, 2026.**
  - B's dividend: 1,200 gross, with 420 Swiss withholding tax (35 %), so 420 refundable.
  - A's reported income: 2.50 per unit × 400 units on 31/12/2026 = 1,000.
  - Taxable income 2,200, income tax estimate 0.30 × 2,200 = 660.
- **Wealth.** 250,000 at 31/12/2026 × 0.003 = 750.
- **Circular 36, 2026.**
  - Value at the start 100,000; purchases 200,000 and sales 150,000 make 350,000 ≤ 500,000:
    met.
  - A sale of units bought 4 months earlier: holding period not met.
  - `net_income` 80,000 with gains of 30,000 (37.5 %): met.
  - Not debt-financed: met. No derivatives: met.
  - Conclusion: professional trading cannot be excluded.
- **2027, all met,** with the sale after 7 months: private wealth management, gains not
  taxed.
- **FIFO holding period:** 100 units bought in January, 100 in August, 150 sold in the next
  March. The units sold are the 100 of January (14 months) and 50 of August (7 months), so
  the minimum is 7 months: met.
- **Not checked:** a year without `net_income` shows criterion 3 as "not checked", and the
  conclusion cannot be "met".
- **Projections:** `income_tax_rate` 0.30 × `taxable_yield` 0.02 = 0.6 % of value a year, by
  hand over two years. Without the keys, the outputs are byte-identical to `main`.
- **Errors:**
  - a base currency other than CHF;
  - `reported_income` for a fund not held on that date, or without a currency;
  - only one of the two projection keys;
  - a `marginal_rate` outside [0, 1).

## Out of scope (to IDEAS)

- Treaty relief for foreign withholding tax (DA-1) and the US additional withholding.
- Securities transfer stamp tax: a broker cost, already a fee in the transactions.
- Real-estate funds (LIFD art. 20 cpv. 1 lett. e) and Swiss pillar 3a.
- German and French reports for Switzerland.
- Reading the ESTV price list automatically: its terms are checked first, and the per-unit
  values stay an input you give.

## Risks

- **This is analytics, not a ruling.** The Circular 36 check says whether the safe haven is
  met; the tax office judges the whole case, and the report says so.
- **Rates are approximations of a progressive tax.** A marginal rate applied to the income
  from this account is an estimate. The report labels it "estimated at your marginal rate".
- **Price-list values are your input.** A wrong per-unit income gives wrong income tax.
  The table shows each value with its date and source, so it can be checked.
