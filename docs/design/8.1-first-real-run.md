# Design note — Phase 8.1: first run on the real portfolio

Status: **draft, waiting for approval.** Deviations, if any, are listed at the end. First phase
of Level 8 (`docs/design/level-8-plan.md`).

## Goal

The owner's real portfolio runs through `portfolio_report`, from the CLI, and the result is
**reconciled with the broker**. Every failure, wrong number and missing feature is written
down. The public part of that list, which contains no personal data, decides what 8.2–8.7
really need.

This phase writes no library code unless a bug blocks the run (see "Bugs").

## Where everything lives (all git-ignored)

```text
data/real/
  inputs/      broker export, price files, holdings files, KID/factsheet notes
  mappings/    broker mapping, price mappings, holdings mappings
  instruments.toml
  checks.toml  a copy of configs/checks/example.toml, with the thresholds you choose
  report.toml  the experiment config
  results/     the run outputs and trials.jsonl
  NOTES.md     private run notes: what failed, the reconciliation, with numbers
```

- **Outputs go under `data/real/results/`, never under `results/`.** `results/studies/` is
  committed, so real outputs stay away from that folder entirely.
- **A check before every commit in this phase.** `git status --ignored` must show
  `data/real/` as ignored, and only synthetic or documentation files may be staged.

## What you provide

1. **The broker export** of all transactions since the account was opened, as the broker's
   CSV or Excel download. Excel is converted to CSV by you or by me; the original is kept
   unchanged.
2. **Prices** for each fund, from a source you use, downloaded by hand. Monthly closing prices
   since the first purchase are enough to start; the type (price or NAV) must be known.
3. **For each fund, the KID and the factsheet** (PDF), or the facts in them: name, ISIN,
   currency, domicile, replication, distribution policy, TER, benchmark, hedging and inception.
   I draft `instruments.toml` from them, and you check it.
4. **The broker's statement** on one recent date: the value of each position and the cash.
5. **The broker's tax statement**: realised gains and losses and the losses balance
   (*zainetto*), for the reconciliation of realised gains.
6. **Optional:** one holdings file per fund, downloaded from the issuer's site. It is expected
   to fail on country names; recording how is part of this phase.

FX comes from the ECB file already in `data/raw/ecb/`. Nothing is downloaded by me.

## Steps

1. **Mappings.**
   - Write the broker mapping in `data/real/mappings/`. Every wording in the export is mapped
     explicitly, and an unknown one raises.
   - Write a price mapping per file, and the instruments TOML.
2. **Validate** with `qrlab validate`, and fix configuration errors.
3. **Run** `qrlab run data/real/report.toml`.
4. **Reconcile, on the statement date:**
   - **value per position:** units × price × FX. A difference is explained by a different
     price or FX source, or by the date, or it is a bug;
   - **cash per currency:** exact to the cent, because cash is booked from the export;
   - **realised gains:** against the broker's tax statement. qrlab uses weighted average cost
     including purchase charges, the *regime amministrato* method, so the two should agree to
     rounding. Any difference is traced to the transactions that cause it.
5. **Add holdings files**, one fund at a time, and record what breaks.
6. **Write the notes:**
   - `data/real/NOTES.md` (private);
   - the public list, `docs/design/8.1-findings.md`.

## The public findings list: what may and may not appear

- **May appear:**
  - the form of a problem: "the broker writes the fee in a separate row", "the issuer file
    uses country names", "the price file has gaps on holidays";
  - which phase it goes to;
  - whether it is a bug.
- **Never appears:**
  - amounts, quantities or dates of your transactions;
  - ISINs or names of the funds you hold;
  - account numbers;
  - the broker's name, unless you say it may.

I show you the list before it is committed.

## Bugs

A bug that blocks the run is fixed on its own branch, with a regression test on synthetic data
that reproduces the same form of input. It goes through the quality gate and is merged after
your OK. Anything that is not a blocker goes into the findings list for a later phase.

## Checks

- The reconciliation in step 4 is the test of this phase. Its numbers are in the private
  notes.
- The public findings list is reviewed by you before the commit.
- `scripts/audit_history.py data/real` runs before any commit made during the phase, to prove
  that no real data reached the history.

## Risks

- **The export does not cover the whole history,** for example transfers in from another
  broker. Then positions and costs are wrong from the start. This must be checked first,
  against the statement's quantities.
- **Price gaps.** `max_price_age_days` decides whether a gap is carried or refused. Its value
  is stated in the notes.
- **Rounding.** The broker rounds per transaction; qrlab keeps exact decimals. A difference of
  a few cents across many transactions is expected and reported as such.

## Effort

About 3–5 h of my work, plus the time you need to collect the files.
