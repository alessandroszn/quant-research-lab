Exploratory work: notebooks and scripts that use `qrlab` but are not part of it.

Rules:
- Code here may be messy; it must not be imported by `src/qrlab`.
- Anything that becomes reusable moves into `src/qrlab` with tests.
- Results here are **exploratory** until rerun through a recorded experiment
  (config in `configs/`, record produced by `qrlab.reporting`) with out-of-sample validation.
