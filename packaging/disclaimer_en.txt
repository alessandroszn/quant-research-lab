Quant Research Lab - research analytics, not investment advice

Quant Research Lab is a research tool. Every number it shows comes from the qrlab library
through the same runner as its command line, with the assumptions stated in each config and
report.

- Results describe data and models. They are not recommendations to buy, sell or hold any
  security, and not personal financial advice.
- In-sample results do not show that a strategy works; projections are distributions of
  outcomes under stated assumptions, not forecasts.
- Your portfolio files stay in the workspace folders you choose. The program does not connect
  to the internet and sends nothing anywhere.

The program is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn before the first start. You can
check the installer against the SHA-256 published with it.

Third-party software and its licences are listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt in the program
folder.
